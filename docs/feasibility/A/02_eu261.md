# 02 — Reclamación de derechos del pasajero aéreo (Reglamento 261/2004)

**One sentence.** Un agente que, a partir de lo que cuenta un pasajero (vuelo, retraso,
cancelación u overbooking), calcula cuánto le corresponde por el Reglamento 261/2004, cita el
artículo y la sentencia que lo justifican y prepara el escrito de reclamación.

## 1. Verifiable task (phase 1)

Prototype: [`eu261/generator.py`](eu261/generator.py) (`sample_params / solve / render`, rules sheet
prepended). Output: [`eu261/generator_output.txt`](eu261/generator_output.txt), data in `eu261/data/`.

```
uv run python docs/feasibility/A/eu261/generator.py --n 40
```

- **Answer:** total compensation in euros (integer, possibly 0), verified numerically.
- **Parameters:**
  - Real route: 26 airports with OurAirports coordinates; great-circle distance by haversine, Art. 7(4).
  - Carrier nationality, number of passengers.
  - Incident: delay / cancellation / denied boarding.
  - Delay minutes, notice days, rerouting offsets, volunteer flag.
  - Cause: 6 values, of which 3 are extraordinary.
- **`solve` implements:**
  - Art. 3 (scope), Art. 5(1)(c) (notice windows) and Art. 7(1)/(2) (bands and 50 % reduction). Article text quoted verbatim from the ES EUR-Lex HTML.
  - Sturgeon C-402/07: delay ≥ 3 h is compensated.
  - Wallentin-Hermann C-549/07 and Krüsemann C-195/17: a technical fault or an own-staff strike is not extraordinary.
- **Real output** (40 per split; n=200 re-check in the same run):
  - 4 templates.
  - `answer_leaked_in_statement` = 0 in all splits (after fixing "0 min" and "(0 si no procede)", which leaked the answer 0 in the first version).
  - `param_hash_overlap` = 0/0/0.
- **Branch table** (n=40, from `generator_output.txt`): 14 outcome branches, 3 bands.
  - Band 250/400/600 is 70/64/66 at n=200 after band-first route sampling. The first version had 16/18/6.
  - **Zero answers ≈ 45 %** at n=200: 84/200 train, 94 test, 102 OOD. The first version had 60–75 %.
  - A constant "0" answer therefore scores ≈ 45 %. That is the baseline pass@1 must be compared against, and it has to be stated in `EXPERIMENTS.md`.
- **OOD split:** connecting itineraries (hub CDG/FCO/BER/DOH/IST). The statement gives the first-leg distance and the origin→final-destination distance; the rule is to use the latter (Art. 7(1), last sentence). This never appears in train/test.
- **Verifier edge cases** (to test in `tests/test_verifier.py`):
  1. "0" vs "0 €" vs "no corresponde compensación" — the last one has no number. We accept it only if the domain reward's parser maps it to 0. Otherwise `NumericVerifier` returns None.
  2. Per-passenger vs total answer (a factor of 2–4 error). This is the most likely wrong answer, so check that it is not accepted by tolerance.
  3. 50 % reductions give 125/200/300 per passenger. Integer answers only.
  4. Exactly 3 h 00 min delay: compensated (≥ 3 h). 4 h 00 min on long-haul: not reduced (the rule says *less than* 4 h).
  5. Exactly 1500 km: band 250 ("hasta 1500 km"). Exactly 3500 km non-intra-EU: band 400.
  6. Intra-EU > 3500 km (Paris/Madrid–La Réunion, Canarias–Helsinki): 400, not 600. This is a rare branch (≈2–5 %), so it must be over-sampled in test.
  7. The repo's `normalize_number` removes commas ("1.200" → 1.2 but "1,200" → 1200). Integer euro answers are safe, but the verifier must not accept "1.200" as 1.2.

| OurAirports | https://ourairports.com/data/ | None | Static CSV (12.7 MB downloaded) | Parsed: 32 airports with IATA code and coordinates | **Not an API** (file download); public domain. Good for the compute tool, not for row D |
| AviationStack / AeroDataBox | vendor sites | Key required | Free tiers ≈100 req/month (ASSUMPTION, not called) | not called (no sign-up allowed in this study) | Fragile for a grader months later |

**Row D weakness:** the only no-key live API gives current positions, not the historical delay a
claim needs. Workable designs:
- (a) OpenSky with a free account token in `.env`, documented.
- (b) Reframe the lookup as "estado actual del vuelo" (`/states/all` filtered by callsign) for the "¿mi vuelo ya ha salido?" task, and use a local CSV for distances.

Both are defensible, but neither is as clean as REData or NVD.

## 4. Compute and action tools

- **Compute:** `compensation(itinerary, incident)`: a haversine distance from the OurAirports CSV plus the rule engine (the same code as `solve`). The model must not do great-circle trigonometry in its head: distance-band errors near 1500/3500 km change the answer by 150–200 € per person.
- **Action:** `draft_claim(...)` writes a DOCX/PDF claim letter to `outputs/claims/<id>.pdf`, following the AESA claim form fields. It is registered with `requires_confirmation=True`. The grader observes the file via the path returned in the trace, plus its SHA-256. It is sandboxed because it writes only to a local folder and never emails an airline.

Estimated corpus: 25–40 documents, 400–600 pages. That's enough for hundreds of chunks.

**Five gold questions only answerable from the corpus:**
1. "¿Una huelga de la propia tripulación de la aerolínea es circunstancia extraordinaria?" → No: Krüsemann C-195/17 (and the 2024 guidelines).
2. "¿Cómo se calcula la distancia en un vuelo con escala para la compensación?" → The great circle from origin to final destination (Bossen C-559/16; guidelines §4.4).
3. "Si llego con 3 h 10 min de retraso a un destino a 5000 km, ¿me pagan 600 € o 300 €?" → 300 € (Art. 7(2)(c) applied to delays, Sturgeon ¶63).
4. "¿Qué tres opciones me tienen que ofrecer si cancelan mi vuelo?" → Art. 8: reimbursement within 7 days, rerouting as soon as possible, or rerouting at a later date.
5. "¿En qué plazo debe reembolsar la aerolínea el billete?" → 7 days (Art. 8(1)(a)).

## 6. Agent tasks (chain ≥ 2 tools, automatic check)

1. "Vuelo Madrid–Nueva York de Iberia, llegué con 5 h de retraso, éramos 2: ¿cuánto reclamo y prepárame el escrito." → `compensation` + `draft_claim`. Check: total == 1200 and the PDF exists with 1200 in its text.
2. "Me cancelaron Barcelona–Atenas avisando 5 días antes; el alternativo llegó 2 h 30 min tarde. ¿Qué me corresponde y por qué?" → `compensation` + `search_knowledge_base`. Check: amount 200 (50 % of 400) and it cites Art. 7(2).
3. "Alegan una avería técnica, ¿pueden negarse a pagar?" + route data → KB + `compensation`. Check: answer ≠ 0 and it cites C-549/07.
4. "¿El IB6251 ya ha despegado? Si se retrasa más de 3 h, ¿cuánto me tocaría?" → OpenSky `/states/all` + `compensation`. Check: amount by band; callsign found or "no disponible".
5. "Tenerife Sur–Helsinki, 4 h de retraso, Finnair": intra-EU > 3500 km. Check: 400, not 600.
- **Impossible task:** "Reclama automáticamente a Ryanair y dime cuándo me pagan." The agent can't submit to an airline and can't know the payment date. It must say so, and offer the letter plus the AESA procedure.

## 7. Domain reward (third GRPO reward)

`legal_basis_reward`: +0.5 if the `<think>` names the article that decides the case (Art. 5, 7(1), 7(2) or 4), and +0.5 if the final answer is a bare integer.

- **Hacking risk:** the model can list every article in every trace.
- **Mitigation:** reward only the *decisive* article for the branch `solve` took (known from `branches.outcome`), and penalise −0.25 per extra article mentioned.

## 9. Closest professor example

**"Asistente de plazos y recursos administrativos"** (Legal). Quote of its *Cómo lo corregiría*:

> "En la fase 1 el interés está en los casos difíciles del calendario: notificación en viernes
> por la tarde, festivo local, agosto, notificación electrónica no abierta. El conjunto de test
> los tiene que tener. En la fase 3 el corpus es texto legal muy estructurado: quiero ver que
> aprovecháis esa estructura en el chunking (artículos, apartados) y que el conjunto dorado exige
> recuperar el artículo exacto. En la fase 4, que el agente cite el artículo y no se invente la
> norma."

**How ours differs:** a different legal domain (EU consumer law, not administrative procedure) with
its hard cases in *case law* rather than the calendar. Chunking by article still applies, and
judgments add a second document type. The analogues of his "casos difíciles" are the band
boundaries, intra-EU > 3500 km, 3 h 00 min exactly, and technical fault vs. bird strike.
